Privacy on this device

Booking Side Notes keeps imported appointment details and side notes in this browser. It does not upload them.

What is stored

The app keeps an appointment ID, name, start and end times, location, and the side notes you create. It needs those fields to show a daily brief while offline.

Calendar files

Your .ics file is read in this browser. The app does not write to your calendar.

Network and tracking

The app has no account, analytics, advertising, background sync, or third-party fonts. It only requests files from this site during normal use.

Your choices

Export a backup before clearing browser storage or moving devices. Clearing this site’s data deletes its local archive. Questions can go to privacy@sociobot.in.
